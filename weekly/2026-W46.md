Nvidia's co-founder Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November amid a scandal involving an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. The news dominated the week and raised questions about Nvidia's role in open-source AI. On the model side, Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 as its IPO roadshow began, and Google released three Gemini products in four days. In Europe, data-protection authorities chose AI in recruitment as their next coordinated enforcement theme.

## Models and products

- **Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2.** Anthropic released both on 9 November. Fable 5.2 went straight to first place on Artificial Analysis. [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/9/llm-anthropic/) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-fable-5-2-retakes-world-1-llm-spot-as-ant-ipo-roadshow-begins/)
- **Gemini 4 Carbon for everyone.** Google's strongest model, which until now only selected cyber defenders could use, is generally available at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-is-now-available/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/llm-gemini/)
- **Gemini 4 Hydrogen.** The lowest-cost Gemini 4 model replaces 3.8 Flash and now powers AI Mode in Google Search. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/) · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/11/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-hydrogen-finally-replacing-3-8-flash/)
- **Decision models arrive.** The Gemini Decisions API costs 10 cents per million input tokens, and output is free. That matches OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna decision model, and both cost more than Jev. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/11/gemini-decisions-api/)
- **Local AI.** Apple is preparing a Mac app that makes running local models easy ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/10/apple-has-new-mac-app-coming-to-make-running-local-ai-models-easy/)). llama.cpp reached v1.0.0 ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llama-cpp-v1)).

Wired puts the market value lost since the scandal began at more than $1 trillion ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-tanked-a-trillion-dollars-in-less-than-a-month/)). - **Funding.** Mercor raised a $750M Series D at a $20B valuation, with a $3B revenue run rate ([Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-mercor-750m-series-d-at-20b-valuation-3b-run-rate/)). Norwest raised a new $4B fund focused on AI ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/norwest-pushes-deeper-into-ai-with-new-4b-fund/)).
- **Credit stress.** CoreWeave's bond yield went above 16% ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/coreweave-bond-yield-tops-16percent-as-ai-credit-stress-rises.html)). SoftBank lost over $3B on gas turbines it pre-ordered for data centres ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/softbanks-billion-dollar-loss-may-have-put-a-cap-on-ais-natural-gas-ambitions/)).
- **AI budgets get capped.** Apple reportedly limited its engineers to $200 of AI use per day. [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/newsletter/2026-11-08-apple-capped-engineers-at-a-200-daily-ai-budget)

## Regulation and policy

- **EDPB: AI in recruitment is the next coordinated enforcement topic.** On 10 November the European Data Protection Board also adopted a statement on South Korea's adequacy and its final recommendations on binding corporate rules. [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/actualite-du-cepd-adequation-de-la-coree-du-sud-versions-finales-des-bcr-st-et-action-coordonnee-sur)
- **AI Act: prohibited practices, and two new bans from 2 December.** An explainer sets out how the Commission reads the eight Article 5 bans. The Digital Omnibus adds two more from 2 December 2026: non-consensual intimate content and AI-generated child sexual abuse material. Fines reach €35M or 7% of worldwide turnover. [EU AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/prohibited-ai-practices-under-the-ai-act/)
- **US: an AI-safety legislator heads to Congress.** Scott Wiener, who wrote California's frontier-AI bills, won Nancy Pelosi's former seat. [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/09/1148182/nancy-pelosis-replacement-is-ready-to-regulate-ai/)
- **AI in schools.** Families called for moratoriums after a Gemini pilot in schools and a teen's lawsuit. [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/families-fight-to-keep-googles-disturbing-ai-off-school-issued-chromebooks/)
- **Labour.** Google DeepMind staff asked the UK government to step into their bid to unionise. [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/google-deepmind-staff-escalate-their-union-fight/)

## Security and safety

- **Fireworks AI breach.** Hackers reached Notion customers' chat transcripts through Notion's third-party AI vendor, Fireworks AI. It was Fireworks' second breach in two months. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/cyberattack-on-fireworks-ai-allowed-hackers-to-access-notion-customers-chat-transcripts/) · [Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/security-incident-involving-a-third-party-ai-vendor)
- **Backdoors in dormant neurons.** Microsoft researchers found "Lazarus", a technique that reprograms dormant neurons so an agent runs malicious commands when a hidden trigger appears. [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/lazare-veni-foras-reprogramming-dormant-neurons-to-backdoor-ai-agents/)
- **Airlines say no to agents.** United Airlines blocked AI agents from booking flights, citing cybersecurity risks. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/united-airlines-blocks-ai-agents-from-booking-flights-citing-cybersecurity-risks/)
- **A kill switch for training runs.** OpenAI described Control Room, which tracks model workloads across its research infrastructure and can stop them quickly. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/control-room)
- **Chatbots send shoppers to fake shops.** A study found AI assistants recommending imposter retail websites. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032020/gemini-chatgpt-perplexity-shopping-test-risky-fake-websites-citations)
