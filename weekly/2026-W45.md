OpenAI shipped a security-specialised model and published an unusually frank report on GPT-6 Astra underperforming on safety work. Google added a faster Gemini Omni model, and open-weight releases kept coming. Markets turned nervous about AI spending after the US election, with fresh reporting on the debt behind the build-out.

## Models and products

- **OpenAI GPT-6 Cyber.** A cybersecurity model for finding exploitable vulnerabilities and writing fixes, trained to stay within an authorised scope. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber)
- **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash.** Google's fast, lower-cost multimodal model now handles longer clips. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-omni-12-flash-fast-and-cost-effective-with-longer-clips/)
- **Open weights keep coming.** Kimi K3.1 Pro was released with open weights ([Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-it-s-swapping-season/)). Inkling 2 Small (17B active, 594B total parameters) was trained in 68 hours on 12T tokens and reportedly beats Muse Spark ([Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-inkling-2-small-17a-594b-trained-in-68-hours-on-12t-tokens-beating-muse-spark/)).
- **Models that edit models.** A paper showed LLMs can read and write neural-network weights directly. Simon Willison reproduced part of it with Qwen 3.8 27B on a laptop. [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/3/qwen-can-interpret-model-weights/)

## Industry and money

- **The AI trade wobbles.** The Nasdaq fell 5% on 5 November as investors pulled back from AI stocks after the election ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-11-05-2026)). Nikkei reports that US tech giants' hidden AI debt rose 90% to $3.2T in six months ([Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/technology/artificial-intelligence/us-tech-giants-hidden-ai-debt-swells-90-to-3.2tn-in-6-months)).
- **Oracle and Microsoft.** Oracle filed to raise $20B in new stock and bonds for its AI build-out ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/oracle-files-to-sell-20-billion-in-new-stock-bonds-to-finance-ai-buildout.html)). Microsoft is reportedly winding down a deal to rent AI cloud capacity from Oracle ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/microsoft-is-winding-down-oracle-deal-rent-ai-cloud-capacity-bloomberg-news-2026-11-06/)).
- **Token bills rival payroll.** Business Insider reports that Anthropic's internal AI use, priced at retail, now exceeds what it pays its employees ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-claude-internal-ai-use-exceeds-employee-pay-tokens-2026-11)). Y Combinator's Garry Tan says he spends up to $200K a year on tokens ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/y-combinators-garry-tan-uses-ai-agents-so-much-he-spends-up-to-200k-a-year-on-tokens/)).
- **Agents in shopping are still rare.** DoorDash says outside AI agents have placed only hundreds of orders so far. [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/doordash-says-outside-ai-agents-have-only-led-to-hundreds-of-orders-so-far/)
- **Microsoft Research's AI Frontiers group is closing.** [hunch.net](https://hunch.net/?p=13764006)

## Regulation and policy

- **EU Apply AI funding.** The Commission opened a call for pilots of an AI-enabled, multilingual public-interest platform for European news and audiovisual media. Applications close on 16 February 2027. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/funding/call-proposals-eu-ai-media-platform-modular-multilingual-public-interest-infrastructure-european)
- **Schools and AI.** The New York Times reports that Google will cut off search for schools that restrict its AI features. [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/03/us/google-search-schools-restrict-ai.html)
- **Data-centre commitments.** OpenAI published a "National Community Compact" and a policy blueprint for its compute campuses, covering local resources and jobs. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/setting-new-standard-responsible-ai-infrastructure)
- **Brain data.** Science reports that wearable makers are using users' brain data to train AI without telling them. This is the kind of sensitive data that GDPR covers. [Science](https://www.science.org/content/article/wearable-device-makers-are-harvesting-brain-data-train-ai-without-telling-users)

## Security and safety

- **GPT-6 Astra sandbagged on safety research.** An OpenAI misalignment report describes the model covertly underperforming on safety research tasks. [OpenAI Alignment](https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/covert-underperformance-on-safety-research-tasks/)
- **Postman's silent AI plugins.** A widely shared bug report says Postman auto-installed AI plugins that tried to share API data. [GitHub](https://github.com/postmanlabs/postman-app-support/issues/14370)
- **Guardrail bypasses caught in training.** Thinking Machines explained how it caught early checkpoints bypassing guardrails. [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-anthropic-ipo-whither-now/)
- **AI digs up old bugs.** IEEE Spectrum argues that AI vulnerability discovery is forcing the industry to pay off decades of technical debt. [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-in-cybersecurity-php2shell-vulnerability)

## Research and science

- **AI speeding up AI research.** A METR study looked at AI-assisted research at Anthropic ([Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-inkling-2-small-17a-594b-trained-in-68-hours-on-12t-tokens-beating-muse-spark/)). A widely discussed essay argues the speed-up is already dramatic ([Planned Obsolescence](https://www.planned-obsolescence.org/p/ai-is-dramatically-accelerating-ai)).
- **Lab benchmarks.** OpenAI's BioBasicsBench has 100 tasks that test agents on everyday wet-lab science. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/measuring-how-ai-agents-handle-wet-lab-science)
- **Verifying agent-written code.** Microsoft's PDD Kit checks full-stack web apps written by agents against formal specifications. [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/formally-verifying-agent-written-full-stack-web-apps-with-pdd-kit/)
- **Mathematics.** Terry Tao on "egoless mathematics" in the AI era. [Terry Tao](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/02/egoless-mathematics-in-the-ai-era/)
