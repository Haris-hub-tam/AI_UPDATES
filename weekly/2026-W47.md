Anthropic went public in the largest IPO on record, and the next day the Nasdaq gave up all its gains for 2026. OpenAI and Google both released new mid-to-frontier models at lower prices. Outages at Outlook, the FAA and US banks led to warnings that AI-speed vulnerability hunting is only starting. In Europe, the CNIL published survey data on young people confiding in chatbots, and privacy regulators worldwide adopted a resolution on AI in education.

## Models and products

- **GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol.** Astra is OpenAI's new frontier model, at $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 cached and $40 output, which is 20% below GPT-6 Astra. Sol is the cheaper everyday model. OpenAI claims Astra leads Agents' Last Exam ahead of Claude Fable 5.2. Early reviews are mixed. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-gpt-6-2-astra-the-2-frontier-model-is-better-and-20-600-cheaper-but-has-mixed-reviews-6-2-sol/)
- **Gemini 4 Neon.** Google's mid-tier model scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, third overall. It costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output, with a 1M-token context window. It is the fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-4-neon/) · [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-gemini-4-neon-gdm-s-opus-sol-contender/)
- **Genie 4** opened to developers. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-is-now-available/)
- **Frontier-ish models on a laptop.** Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights multimodal looped transformer that runs offline on a 32GB MacBook Pro. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/thinking-machines-wows-with-inkling-2-mini-looped-transformer-model-that-runs-locally-on-laptops) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/inkling2mini)
- **Agents that use your computer.** Gemini can now operate a Mac ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/google-gives-gemini-the-ability-to-use-your-mac-for-you/)), and Apple may be building its own Mac agent ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/apple-may-be-developing-its-own-ai-agent-that-runs-your-mac/)).
- **Hardware.** At SC26, Nvidia announced the Rubin HPC GPU and topped MLPerf Training with Vera Rubin. [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/mlperf-training-endpoints-vera-rubin/)

## Industry and money

- **Anthropic lists on Nasdaq.** The IPO was priced at $83, above the initial range. The shares closed their first day at $97.17, up 17%, valuing Anthropic at about $1.5T. Sources differ on how much the offering raised, but all call it the largest IPO on record. [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-anthropic-successfully-completes-largest-ipo-in-history-valued-at-1-5t/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)
- **Then the sell-off.** On 20 November the Nasdaq erased all of its 2026 gains as an AI sell-off hit Anthropic's second trading day. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/nov/20/global-stock-markets-slump-anthropic-ipo-ai-sell-off)
- **Nvidia after Huang.** Earnings still showed healthy growth while executives lobbied for Jensen Huang's return. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/21/a-tale-of-two-nvidias/)
- **Cost discipline.** Uber went over its AI budget and scaled back context and reasoning in its coding tools. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/uber-blew-through-its-ai-budget-then-dialed-back-context-and-reasoning-in-its-ai-coding-tools)
- **Data-centre politics.** Trump threatened a "tremendous tax" on companies that build data centres outside the US ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1038892/trump-foreign-data-centers-tax)). Meta is "re-evaluating" its 2030 net-zero goal after record emissions from AI data centres ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/meta-reconsiders-2030-net-zero-goal-amid-record-emissions-from-ai-data-centers/)).

## Regulation and policy

- **CNIL: young people confide in AI.** In a survey published with Groupe VYV, 48% of 11–25 year-olds in France said they use AI tools to talk about intimate subjects. Of those who ask AI for personal advice, 64% see it as a "life adviser". The CNIL frames this as both a mental-health and a privacy issue. [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/ia-conversationnelle-la-voix-des-jeunesses)
- **Global privacy regulators on AI in education.** The 48th Global Privacy Assembly met in Brussels on 16–18 November. Its more than 130 authorities adopted five resolutions, two of them on AI, and a strategic plan for 2027–2030. The education resolution, which the CNIL helped draft, sets out 11 principles built on the best interests of the child. [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/48e-gpa) · [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/gpa-ia-education)
- **Commission: Apply AI Summit.** Nearly 1,000 policymakers, start-ups and industry leaders met in Brussels on AI uptake across ten strategic sectors and the public sector. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-hosts-apply-ai-summit-boost-europes-ai-innovation-and-uptake)
- **EDU-EDIC launched.** The Commission and eight member states, France among them, created a European Digital Infrastructure Consortium for education. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/european-digital-infrastructure-consortium-launches-today-strengthen-digital-sovereignty-education)
- **No deal on "killer robots".** UN talks in Geneva ended without agreed rules on autonomous weapons. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1039554/un-regulations-killer-robots)
- **Coming up: two new AI Act bans apply from 2 December 2026**, on non-consensual intimate content and AI-generated child sexual abuse material. [EU AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/prohibited-ai-practices-under-the-ai-act/)

## Security and safety

- **A week of outages.** A multi-day Outlook outage, a nationwide FAA ground stop and banking failures at JPMorgan Chase all hit in the same week. Experts tie this to companies patching faster as attackers use AI to find vulnerabilities. [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/brace-yourself-for-years-of-outage-chaos/) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1039851/major-tech-outages)
- **An 11-minute zero-day.** Amazon says attackers' AI agents found and exploited a flaw in MONAI Label, an open-source medical-imaging tool, in about 11 minutes. A fix is merged, but there is no patched release yet. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/security/amazon-says-attackers-ai-agents-found-and-exploited-a-zero-day-in-11-minutes-but-no-patched-release-is-available)
- **AI routers leak data.** Researchers planted 2 million canary tokens in prompts sent through AI gateways and brokers, and found sensitive data leaking widely. [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/a-leaky-ai-supply-chain-is-exposing-secret-user-data-at-an-alarming-rate/) · [Dragos](https://www.dragos.com/blog/ai-supply-chain-security-canary-tokens)
- **Agents and private data.** Employees say Meta's Muse agent showed them other people's conversations and data ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-mixing-up-conversations-and-exposing-private-data-report-says/)). Signal now stops AI assistants built into Android and iOS from reading its screen ([Signal](https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/)). US banks are banning third-party AI agents from logging into customer accounts ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/banks-are-cracking-down-on-ai-agents/)).
- **OpenAI on risk.** OpenAI described how it manages frontier risk from training to deployment and how it counters adversarial distillation. It is also working with NATO cyber agencies. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/managing-frontier-ai-risk) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/strengthening-nato-allied-cyber-defences-ai)

## Research and science

- **Two claimed proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis.** Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge says he proved it, and the generalised version, working with Claude. Separately, a human-written proof by Alessandro Masullo has been under expert review for months. Neither is verified yet. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1010051/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-millennium-prize-problem) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)
- **METR's time-horizon graph updated.** Some capabilities are still improving exponentially, but models remain weaker on tasks that need judgement. [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/20/1149399/the-most-misunderstood-graph-in-ai-just-got-a-major-update/)
- **AI in chip design.** OpenAI models optimised circuits and improved RISC-V core designs ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models)). A start-up claims an AI designed and verified a whole new CPU architecture ([Architect Labs](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite)).
- **Drug discovery benchmark.** OpenAI's MedChemBench tests how models reason about molecules and select compounds. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-medchembench)
